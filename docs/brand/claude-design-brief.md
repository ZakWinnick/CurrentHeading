## Company name

Current Heading

## Company blurb

Current Heading is films, photographs, and field notes by Zak Winnick, covering
EV ownership, DC fast charging, and aviation. It goes to the charger, the ramp,
and the cockpit, and reports what is actually there. The camera usually notices
first.

## Design system name

Sectional Noir

## Other notes

### Palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|------|-----|------|
| Ramp Black | `#0E0A08` | Canvas. Warm near black, never pure black |
| Hangar | `#171310` | Raised surface, used sparingly |
| Sectional Line | `#342F2C` | Hairlines, borders, structural line work |
| Oxide | `#DD6836` | Accent. Under 10 percent of visual weight |
| Chart Paper | `#F0EAE3` | Primary text |
| Dusk Bone | `#CFCAC2` | Secondary text |

### Font pairing

Archivo for display at 600 and 700, Manrope for body at 400 to 600. JetBrains
Mono is reserved for dates, durations, and compact technical labels, never for
running text. If a single pairing is needed, use Archivo with Manrope.

### Tone words

Precise. Restrained. Observant. Blunt. Unimpressed by marketing.

### Do and do not

**Do** let neutrals carry the page and let one real photograph do the work of a
whole section. Structure over decoration. Every visual element should look like
it was measured before it was drawn.

**Do** keep Oxide scarce. It marks actions and metadata. The moment it fills a
section it stops meaning anything.

**Do not** use em dashes anywhere, in any copy. Use periods, commas, "and", or
restructure the sentence. This is a hard rule across the whole brand.

**Do not** add decorative looping motion, marquees, scanlines, bounce, or
ornamental gradients. Hover and focus transitions may use short ease out
transforms and must honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Do not** generate photographs of chargers, aircraft, vehicles, or people.
Photography is Zak's own work and that is the point of the channel. Generated
imagery is limited to abstract texture, pattern, and atmosphere.

### Visual grammar

Charge behaviour drawn in the language of aeronautical charts. A charge curve
and a topographic contour are the same gesture. Load falloff reads as an
isobar. Airspace boundaries and connector geometry share one line weight. One
grammar covers both halves of the channel, which is what the name earns.
